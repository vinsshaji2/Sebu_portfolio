S2 — About me

The art of making people feel

Cinema, to me, is the art of making people feel.

What began as creating videos with friends in school grew into a passion for transforming ideas into stories that connect with audiences. Today, that passion has taken me from assisting on commercial productions and campaign films to directing my own short film — each experience reinforcing my love for the craft of filmmaking.

On set, I’m known for staying composed, finding practical solutions, and contributing creatively wherever needed. I’m currently building my journey as an Assistant Director while working toward my goal of directing feature films that entertain, resonate, and leave audiences talking long after they’ve watched them.
